# IsekaiSlop Game Design

## Core fantasy

The player enters the world as nobody and gradually becomes one of the strongest and most prestigious adventurers alive. Progression must be visible both mechanically and visually.

## First-player tutorial vertical slice

### 1. Spawn and race

- The player initially spawns with a random race.
- Race changing/rerolling is a later system.
- Players will eventually be able to change race either through farmed in-game resources or Robux by speaking to a dedicated NPC.

### 2. Tavern registration

- A clear tutorial arrow directs the new player to the tavern.
- A woman NPC stands behind the bar and acts as the player's first guide.
- Speaking to her starts Adventurer Registration.
- A dedicated registration menu opens.
- The player chooses a starting class.
- The choice is confirmed with an adventurer ID/card visual and an official stamp animation.

### 3. Quest board introduction

- After registration, a tutorial arrow directs the player to the quest billboard.
- The board should visibly display exciting higher-rank quests to create aspiration and make the world feel larger than the tutorial.
- During the first tutorial, only the F-Rank goblin quest can actually be chosen.
- The Accept Quest button is strongly highlighted.

### 4. First quest: Goblin Hunt

Objective: kill goblins and their master in a cave.

- The player enters a semi-open forest/mountain adventure zone.
- Ambient creatures such as rabbits and slimes make the zone feel alive.
- The main quest remains the goblin cave objective.
- A compass is displayed at the top of the screen.
- A quest marker indicates the direction of the objective.
- During this first quest only, stronger tutorial arrows may also be used so new players cannot get lost.

### 5. Cave encounter

- Small goblins appear outside/near the cave entrance.
- The player fights through them first.
- A visually distinct Goblin Master appears as the tutorial boss.
- Defeating the Goblin Master completes the quest.

### 6. Return to the capital

- Quest completion triggers an animated teleport sequence.
- The player returns to a teleporter positioned in front of the tavern.
- Goblins drop green crystals as their key early-game loot.

### 7. First reward and mount introduction

- The player returns to the tavern woman.
- The green crystals are shown/sold as part of the reward flow.
- She congratulates the player and reinforces that they are stronger than expected for a new adventurer.
- She explains that the world is large and travelling on foot will be inconvenient.
- The player is directed to the ranch/stable NPC.
- The first horse can be bought using money earned from the goblin quest.

## After the tutorial

Expand the world gradually with:

- weapon merchant
- blacksmith
- armour/equipment
- potion/alchemy shop
- jobs/professions
- fishing
- crafting
- more quest ranks
- more zones
- bosses and dungeons
- mounts
- titles
- awakenings
- parties and co-op

## Wider game loop

Capital city -> choose quest/bounty -> prepare gear/skills -> travel to adventure zone -> explore/fight/events/boss -> earn XP/gold/loot -> return to capital -> upgrade -> increase Adventurer Rank -> unlock harder content.

## Adventurer progression

- Adventurer Rank: F -> E -> D -> C -> B -> A -> S
- Character progression includes level, stats, class/combat style, skills, equipment rarity, upgrades, titles, mounts and later awakenings.
- Progress should become increasingly visually impressive through stronger armour, weapons, effects, auras, titles, mounts and transformations.

## World direction

The capital acts as the permanent social/progression hub. Adventure areas should feel semi-open and explorable, with hidden locations, elites, rare spawns, NPCs, treasure, random events, caves/dungeons and bosses.

Keep the visual language coherent, original medieval fantasy with anime-RPG energy. Avoid obvious derivative copies of existing games/IP.
